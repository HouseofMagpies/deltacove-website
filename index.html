How we think about the work

A safe place for change.

Transparency by design.

Understanding starts with being able to see inside. We expose the workings of knowledge systems from the outset, helping people manage complexity, understand how information is used and address security. Systems need room to breathe and evolve.

A consistent core. Room to evolve.

AI blurs the boundary between software and data platforms. A visible data core provides continuity, with room for new information and whichever tools the work needs: scheduled scripts, bounded agents or guided human contributions.

Agentic sceptical. Agentic hopeful.

Agents open up new ways of working. We see them as mission-specific, bounded parts of an ecosystem of people, process and data. Their contribution should be visible, reviewable and connected to the wider work.
